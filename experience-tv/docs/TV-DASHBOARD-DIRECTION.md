# The television as an instrument panel (direction note, 15 November 2026)

The owner asked that the Botanic television feel like the monitor of a premium electric car (reference: Polestar), without a radical change of the approved layout. This note lists what is taken from that world and what is not.

## What Polestar's displays do (public sources, read 15.11.2026)
- The displays aim to show what the driver needs and nothing more ([Polestar 4 technology](https://www.polestar.com/uk/polestar-4/technology/)).
- A small set of display modes; the calmest shows only speed and battery ([Polestar 3 infotainment](https://www.polestar.com/uk/polestar-3/infotainment)).
- Scandinavian minimalism, one large centre screen, few controls; for 2026 Polestar is bringing some physical buttons back because pure touch screens were criticised ([Carscoops, Feb 2026](https://www.carscoops.com/2026/02/polestar-wants-to-add-more-buttons-into-its-cars/)).

## What this means for the television
| Car | Television | Status |
|---|---|---|
| The panel wakes up when the car starts | Ignition: dark glass, line of light, BOTANIC, two readouts (100% Natural Latex, Dunlop Technology), the stage lights up | Built (preview), test R29 |
| Calm mode: only the essentials | Your selection = the instrument cluster: four readouts (size, firmness, depth, cover) and the price; everything else stays in the topics | Already so; no change |
| Few physical controls, always in the same place | 14 topic buttons in two rows, the left rail, Add to Bag in one place | Already so; no change |
| One accent colour for "active" | Graphite-blue for current, lit blue only for the action (Add to Bag / Go to Checkout, gallery) | Already so |
| Technical readouts in small caps with a large value | Labels in small caps, values large (e.g. depth cards "24 cm · 9.5 inches") | Kept; extend only where a screenshot already shows it |

Not taken: dark full-screen stages (the owner rejected them), glow, neon or extra icons, any new photograph or logo.
