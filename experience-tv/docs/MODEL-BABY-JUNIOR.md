## 2. What must be settled before any live step

These are the draft's own open points. They are not formalities: a children's mattress goes live only when the owner has them confirmed in writing.

1. **Firmness:** the provisional Medium is confirmed by the owner, or replaced by a tested value.
2. **Safety testing** of the finished product: confirmed, with the report reference.
3. **Water-resistant protector** and its compliance: confirmed for baby-cot use.
4. **Cot and bed compatibility** for each size and height: stated per combination.
5. **Prices** per size and height. The store has none yet.
6. **Approved text** for every panel (the draft text above is a development draft, not approved wording).
