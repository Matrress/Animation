Dual Plush system

What is Dual Plush?

A complete sleep system: a latex mattress and a separate latex topper. The mattress gives the main support; the topper refines comfort and pressure relief.

Two firmness layers work together, so the final feel can be matched more closely to body weight, proportions and sleeping preference.

Common combinations

  • Medium mattress + Soft topper
  • Firm mattress + Medium topper
  • Extra Firm mattress + Firm topper
