A complete sleep system: a latex mattress and a separate latex topper. The latex mattress gives the main support; the topper refines comfort and pressure relief.

Two firmness layers work together, so the final feel can be matched more closely to body weight, proportions and sleeping preference.

Natural Latex: from natural origin to a complete sleep system

Natural latex begins as rubber-tree sap, a renewable plant-based material, collected and then turned into resilient latex foam through the Dunlop process. Our latex mattresses and toppers are built on 100% natural latex rubber foam.
