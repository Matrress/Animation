Questions customers ask

How is my firmness decided?

Your body weight sets the starting point and your height refines it, using Dunlop's regulation rules. Your answers then decide which model comes first. They never lower the support your weight needs.

Which firmness do I need at 110 kg?

From 100 kg the regulation is Extra Firm. Orthopaedic Coconut Coir is the first choice, and Botanic Dual Plush (an Extra Firm mattress with a Firm topper) is the second choice for 100 to 114 kg.

Why is Orthopaedic Coconut Coir first for Extra Firm?

Extra Firm is the highest structural regulation. Orthopaedic Coconut Coir is made for it: a coconut-coir reinforced construction with an Extra Firm face. Botanic in Extra Firm is the classic latex alternative.

Can two people share one mattress?

Yes. Each person is regulated on their own body. Mattress for Partners gives each side its own firmness: Medium with Firm, Medium with Extra Firm, or Firm with Extra Firm. A Soft side cannot be combined in it.

Is this a medical recommendation?

No. A mattress supports comfort and does not treat medical conditions. If you have one, ask your doctor.
