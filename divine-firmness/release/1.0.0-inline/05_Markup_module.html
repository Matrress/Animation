    How to judge a latex mattress: lie on it, do not sit on it

    A latex mattress can only be judged lying down. When you sit, your weight concentrates in one small area and the latex cannot absorb it properly. Lying down spreads your weight over the whole surface, so the body rests on top of the latex mattress, the way it does all night.

    The balance between adaptation and support

    We look at one thing: the balance between anatomical adaptation and orthopaedic support. One cannot work without the other. A latex mattress that is too soft lets the body sink and gives no support. A latex mattress that is only hard cannot adapt, so the muscles cannot let go and the nervous system cannot relax. Circulation and sleep then become less complete.
