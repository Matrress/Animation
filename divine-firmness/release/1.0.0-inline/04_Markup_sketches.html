Not too hard. Not too soft. Adapted and supported.

Lying on your side, your shoulder and hip must sink in while your waist is carried. Only then does the spine stay straight.

Too firm

Body rests on two points, shoulder and hip. The waist hangs free and the spine arches. Support is not spread, it is concentrated.

Too soft

The hips sink deepest and the whole body hangs in a hammock. The spine bends under and nothing carries it.

Adapted and supported

Shoulder and hip sink exactly as far as they need, the waist is carried and the spine stays straight. This is the effect Dunlop regulation looks for.
