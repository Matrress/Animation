Not too hard. Not too soft. Adapted and supported.

Lying on your side, your shoulder and hip must sink in while your waist is carried. Only then does the spine stay straight.

Too firm

The body rests on the shoulder and the hip only. The waist is not carried, the spine arches and the support is concentrated on two points.

Too soft

The hips sink further than the shoulders and the body hangs in a hollow. The spine bends under, so nothing carries the pelvis.

Adapted and supported

Shoulder and hip sink exactly as far as they need and the waist is carried. The spine stays straight. This is the balance Dunlop regulation looks for, the one in the model above.
