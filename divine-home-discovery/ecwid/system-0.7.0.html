Comfort & Regulation

Choosing firmness online should not be a guess. In our system it is a rule.

Arranged for certainty. For us firmness is not a word on a label. It is the measured balance between the support the latex gives and the way it adapts to the body, calculated for each body weight. Engineering, not marketing.

  1. Start With Your Body Weight

    Your weight points to one support range. Follow the range for your body weight and your choice rests on engineering.

    • Softup to 48 kg
    • Medium47–83 kg
    • Firm85–110 kg
    • Extra Firmfrom 120 kg

  2. Choose Your Depth

    Every model comes in real, measured depths. Depth is chosen after firmness, never instead of it.

    • Botanic20 cm · 24 cm · 26 cm
    • Botanic Dual Plush23 cm · 18 + 5 · 28 cm · 20 + 8
    • Bio Comfort23 cm · 26 cm
    • Bio Comfort Dual Plush25 cm · 20 + 5 · 28 cm · 20 + 8
    • Orthopaedic Coconut Coir17 cm · 20 cm · 25 cm
    • Ambient16 cm · 18 cm · 20 cm · 26 cm
    • Mattress for Partners21 cm · 2 × 16 + 5 · 25 cm · 2 × 20 + 5

  3. Between Two Firmnesses? Use Dual Plush

    Dual Plush combines two firmnesses in one system: the latex mattress follows the body-weight rule, and a latex topper on top follows the comfort you prefer. The support stays right for you, and the comfort is yours to choose.

  4. Two Sleepers, Two Regulations

    Mattress for Partners gives each partner a core regulated for their own body weight (Medium + Firm, Medium + Extra Firm, Firm + Extra Firm) under one shared latex topper.

Made to Order. Every model is built to the size, firmness, depth and cover you confirm. These are selection guidelines, not medical rules.
