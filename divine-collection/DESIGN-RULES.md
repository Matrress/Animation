# Design rules of the owner (apply to every version, every change)

1. **Cubism.** Where there is a choice, organise in rounded cubes with equal gaps (tiles, pictures, texts, facts, steps, headings, buttons). The eye sorts blocks logically.
2. **Capital letters always carry spacing.** Every uppercase text has letter-spacing of at least 0.1em (checked by `tests/interaction.mjs`, section 7b).
3. **Every model has its own family label above it, and it appears once.** Premium above Botanic and again above Botanic Dual Plush; the same for every family, even when two models share it. The label is a small cube in the family colour (text only) directly above the card; it is not repeated inside the card.
4. **Colour is an edge, never a mass.** The family colour is a thin bar under the picture inside each card. Above the tiles nothing is coloured except the family word itself (no edge, no line, no dash). Nothing coloured along the top of a card.
5. **Clean and light, like the Tesla web store.** White, airy, flat; not dark.
6. **Button text:** "View model & buy" (a short "View & buy" where the button is a small chip). Sea-blue, never an electric blue or a green.
7. **Schemes show each variation**, small, one per combination, coloured like the labels beside them. One scheme must never stand for all variations. A scheme is a glance, not a lecture.
8. **Text must read clearly:** dark, large enough, on solid boxes, no see-through layers behind text.
9. **Rows of mattresses are clearly separated** (gap and a divider bar) so a heading never reads as belonging to the row above.
10. **Organic badge** (leaf + word) is a clear mark in the preview card, not a faint watermark behind text.
11. No prices; no "Firm for Him / Soft for Her"; no "No layers"; no unverified product facts or medical claims.
12. **Badge word follows the product:** "Organic" in the preview card, but "Natural" on Ambient and Hotel Line (the Best Value models).
13. **Preview card text is near-black (#0b0f14), at least 16.5 px for reading text, on solid boxes.** Contrast and clarity before decoration.
14. **Every model shows its function line** (what it is for) in the small card and in the big card, with the same words. It is an orientation, not decoration.

15. **Every model states what it is** ("... latex mattress") above its other lines, in the small and the big card, so a customer is always sure they get a latex mattress; this also serves SEO. Older key lines are never deleted: they stay below the newer lines (where space allows in the small card, always in the big card).
