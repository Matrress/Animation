# Design rules of the owner (apply to every version, every change)

1. **Cubism.** Where there is a choice, organise in rounded cubes with equal gaps (tiles, pictures, texts, facts, steps, headings, buttons). The eye sorts blocks logically.
2. **Capital letters always carry spacing.** Every uppercase text has letter-spacing of at least 0.1em (checked by `tests/interaction.mjs`, section 7b).
3. **A label appears once.** The family name (Premium, Essential, ...) sits only in the heading above its tiles, in the family colour. It is not repeated inside the tiles (phones, where headings are hidden, are the only exception).
4. **Colour is an edge, never a mass.** The family colour is a thin edge: under the heading, and in the lower part of each tile (not along its top, so the space above the tile stays clear).
5. **Clean and light, like the Tesla web store.** White, airy, flat; not dark.
6. **Button text:** "View model & buy" (a short "View & buy" where the button is a small chip). Sea-blue, never an electric blue or a green.
7. **Schemes show each variation**, small, one per combination, coloured like the labels beside them. One scheme must never stand for all variations. A scheme is a glance, not a lecture.
8. **Text must read clearly:** dark, large enough, on solid boxes, no see-through layers behind text.
9. **Rows of mattresses are clearly separated** (gap and a divider bar) so a heading never reads as belonging to the row above.
10. **Organic badge** (leaf + word) is a clear mark in the preview card, not a faint watermark behind text.
11. No prices; no "Firm for Him / Soft for Her"; no "No layers"; no unverified product facts or medical claims.
