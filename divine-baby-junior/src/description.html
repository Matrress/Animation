A natural environment for your baby

From 100% natural latex.

Your baby’s mattress is made of two natural parts only: a core of 100% natural Dunlop latex and a cover of 100% cotton. There are no other elements inside, and no glued layers.

  • 100% natural latex core. Your baby sleeps on Dunlop latex from the rubber tree, moulded as one piece.
  • 100% cotton cover. A natural fibre, next to your baby’s skin, quilted in wide waves.
  • A clean, hypoallergenic sleep environment. Made only from natural latex and cotton, with no other elements, and tested for harmful substances.
  • A core that breathes. Open cells and ventilation channels let air move through your baby’s mattress, not only across its top.
  • A cover that comes off. The zip along the side opens the cover. It comes off and washes at 40 °C.
  • OEKO-TEX® Standard 100. Tested for harmful substances, for baby use.

Contains natural rubber latex. If anyone in your household has a latex allergy, please seek medical advice before you buy.

“Hypoallergenic” and the OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 statement are the owner’s: the certificate (number, product class, holder and validity) must name this product and its components before publication, and natural latex is a known allergen for some people. “Washes at 40 °C” and “no other elements” (including any wadding inside the quilting) are also to be confirmed with the manufacturer.

Comfort for your baby

Stable support. Natural adaptation.

  • Support and adaptation need each other. Support comes from density. Adaptation comes from elasticity. Your baby needs both, well balanced, and latex gives them together.
  • Your baby stays on top. Your baby does not sink into the mattress. Your baby rests on it, always, free to turn and move.
  • Instant adaptation. No delay, as there is with memory foam. The latex answers the moment your baby touches it, which helps your baby sleep more comfortably.
  • A calmer night, for your baby and for you. A good sleeping environment helps your baby settle, and a settled baby means more rest for you.
  • Ventilation for your baby. Children need temperature comfort as much as adults do. Warmth and moisture are what microbes like: the more air moves through the core, the less welcoming it is to them.
  • Medium, for a growing child. A growing body needs steady, even support. Medium is chosen to balance your baby’s weight and the support underneath, from the first cot to the junior bed.

Which depth should I choose?

Start with your cot or bed. Many cots state a maximum mattress depth; choose a depth within it. Baby Cot comes in 10 and 12 cm, Junior in 14 and 16 cm, and a deeper mattress is not automatically a better one.

What is the difference between Baby Cot and Junior?

Baby Cot is for your baby’s cot or cot bed, in four sizes and two depths, 10 and 12 cm. Junior is for your child’s own bed, in six sizes and two depths, 14 and 16 cm. Both are made from the same 100% natural latex core and 100% cotton cover.

What is the cover made of?

The cover is 100% cotton fabric, quilted in wide transverse waves, with a zip along the side.

How do I know the size will fit?

Measure the inside of the frame, length and width, and choose the matching size. The mattress should fit snugly with no gaps at the sides or ends.

Why only one firmness?

The Baby & Junior range is made in a single firmness, Medium, in every size and depth. A growing body needs steady, even support, and Medium is chosen to balance your baby’s weight with the support underneath.
