01 · Construction

Open the cover and the whole mattress is in view

Under the cotton cover there is one block of latex. No foam layers, no coir and no adhesive seams: what you see when the zip is open is the complete construction.

Step 1

Measure the inside of the cot or bed frame, length and width, not the outside.

Step 2

Choose the size that matches. The mattress should fit snugly, with no gaps at the sides or ends.

Step 3

Check the cot manufacturer’s instructions for the mattress size and maximum height they specify.

These are proposed sizes, not confirmed cot compatibility. 60 × 120 and 70 × 140 are the common UK cot and cot-bed sizes; 60 × 110, 70 × 130 and 70 × 160 need a real market check. Verify the permitted gap under current UK guidance before publishing any gap figure.

05 · Firmness

Medium

Provisional specification

The intended firmness of the finished mattress is Medium. It will be confirmed by testing the finished product before the range is released.

SofterMediumFirmer

Our adult firmness recommendations are based on body weight and sleeping position. They do not apply to babies and young children and are not used for this range.

Highest-priority item. UK safer sleep guidance asks for a firm, flat mattress for babies, and BS EN 16890 includes a firmness test. “Medium” must be tested and may need to become “Firm” for Baby Cot sizes. Do not publish until the test result is in.

06 · Care & compatibility

Before it goes in the cot

Fit

Use the mattress only in a cot or bed whose inner dimensions match the size you ordered, and within the maximum height given by the cot manufacturer.

Unpacking

Remove all packaging, including any plastic wrapping, before use, and keep it away from children.

Bedding

Use a fitted sheet made for the exact mattress size. Keep the sleeping surface flat and clear.

Cover

Care instructions for the cotton cover are on the label sewn into the cover. Please follow them.

Contains natural rubber latex

If anyone in your household has a latex allergy, please seek medical advice before purchase.

Safer sleep

Always follow current safer sleep guidance from the NHS and The Lullaby Trust, together with the instructions supplied with your cot.

Washability, waterproofing and cleaning temperature are deliberately not stated. Confirm the sewn-in care label exists and what it says. Confirm whether a mattress protector is recommended, and which type, before adding it.
